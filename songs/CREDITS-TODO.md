# Song credits to review

The build shows credits from `{meta: original ...}`, `{artist: ...}`, `{meta: translator ...}` and
`{copyright: ...}` in each `songs/NNN-*.cho` (see AGENTS.md). Only verified credits are filled in:
songs 23, 52, 120, 173, 236 and 239.

Do not take authors from spodelenohvalenie.com: its attributions are mostly wrong (often the church that
recorded or translated the song). It is only useful when it names an English original, and even then
the original should be checked.
